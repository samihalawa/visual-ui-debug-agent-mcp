# Code of conduct

VUDA contributors are expected to keep project discussions welcoming, constructive, and focused on the work.

- Be respectful when reviewing ideas and code.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Do not harass, insult, discriminate against, or expose private information about others.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about scope and release readiness.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
